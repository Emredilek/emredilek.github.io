FiveOut: Basketball Manager
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

DIEM develops FiveOut: Basketball Manager (Android package com.diem.fiveout). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game or contact us for support.

Information within the game

The current game does not require an online account and does not transmit your gameplay data or manager profile to DIEM. Manager names, career settings, tactical plans and game progress are stored locally on your device. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information as a manager name or in game notes.

The current game does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics services or developer-operated cloud saving. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or camera images.

Purchases and Google Play

FiveOut is a paid game distributed through Google Play. Google handles store purchases, pre-registration, payment processing and its own platform services under its policies. DIEM does not receive your payment card details. Google Play may make order information available to DIEM for purchase support and refunds.

Device or operating-system backup services may back up app data according to your device settings. These services are operated by their respective providers.

Support correspondence

If you contact DIEM, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots or order reference. We use it to answer your request and resolve the reported issue. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.

Support correspondence may be processed by our email service provider to deliver and store messages. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law.

Storage and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until it is replaced or removed. You can clear it through Android's app storage settings or uninstall the game. Clearing local data deletes the associated progress. Manage any operating-system backup copies through your device or backup provider.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided, subject to those obligations.

Security

The game uses the platform's app storage for local saves. We take reasonable measures to protect information supplied to us for support, but no storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

Children

FiveOut is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. The current game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information through a support request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
